Final Summary

Market Mist began with a simple question
        What is price?


By the end of this book, the answer is not a formula.

It is a way of understanding the market.

Price is not merely a number.

It is the result of value, expectations, capital flows, market sentiment, and human behavior
interacting with one another.

Price is not the answer.

It is where the question begins.

To understand price, it is not enough to watch whether it rises or falls.

You must observe:

   Market Structure.
   Trading Volume.
   Price location.
   Trend.
   Whether the market has accepted the price.
   Whether capital flows support the move.
   Whether market sentiment has become overheated.
   Whether risk remains under control.
   Whether you have maintained your discipline.

The true message of this book is this:

Investing is not about finding certainty.

It is about building a repeatable process in an uncertain world.

No investor can control the market.

But every investor can control:

       Whether to wait for confirmation.
       Whether to write a trading plan.
       Whether to set a stop-loss.
       Whether to manage Position Size.

        Whether to calculate the Risk–Reward Ratio.
        Whether to avoid emotional trading.
        Whether to conduct an honest post-trade review.
        Whether to turn losses into new trading rules.

The market will always contain noise.

But a disciplined process prevents you from being overwhelmed by it.

The market will always present temptation.

But discipline prevents you from mistaking temptation for opportunity.

The market will always produce mistakes.

But continuous review ensures those mistakes are never wasted.

That is the truth behind price.

It is not a secret.

It is not a shortcut.

It is not a magical indicator.

It is simply this:

         Understand Market Structure.
         Respect risk.
         Execute your plan.
         Review continuously.

May everyone who reaches the final page of this book leave with more than the ability to read a
chart.

May you also learn how to remain clear-minded whenever you stand before the market.
